# YouTube Upload Tool

Developer: Maxim Peshkov — peshkov1.max@gmail.com
Google Cloud project: extreme-lodge-504919-f7

YouTube Upload Tool is a small, free, single-user utility. It automates one job for its
owner: publishing the long-form relaxing-piano music videos he produces on his own computer to his own
YouTube channel, and managing their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status through the official
YouTube Data API v3: https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3.

The tool has no users other than its owner, no registration, no accounts, no backend, no server, no
advertising and no analytics. It runs locally and talks only to Google's own API endpoints over HTTPS.
It requests the three YouTube scopes it needs (youtube.upload, youtube,
youtube.readonly) and nothing else; it never reads other people's channels, viewer data,
comments, or any Google data outside the owner's own uploads.
